## Source waist reference

reference_cm is the saved released-source **raw contour perimeter**, loaded by E4 site_record, retained in E2 record_branch, carried by WAIST discover, and read by WAIST paired through valid_value(reference, 'raw_contour_cm'). It is not an independent tape measurement, nor the new E2 midpoint proxy.

Initial site selection is data.nomo_released_metrology.measure: mapped umbilicus/chest anchors, lower-half adaptive selector select_adaptive(profile, 0.5, 0.01), and a +0.005 m umbilical fallback when unresolved. The final source values also include data.nomo_contact_sections.correct_trunk with SourceSeamSections.section, and SurfaceCycleSections.section for recovered failed contours. These remeasure raw perimeters at retained planes and preserve the historical selector flags. The released contour-engine code and [trace](waist-reference-audit.json) make that distinction explicit: a selector fallback is different from a contour-recovery operation. MHR-derived anchor projection makes the reference dependent.

All 596 linked source/pathway/state entries in the exact 149-person complete cohort were compared with their saved detail files and the paired reference_cm values. No value was regenerated or substituted. Source flags occur in 56/149 SMPL-NICP and 149/149 CAESAR-NICP observations. Body flags occur in 113/149 SMPL canonical, 62/149 SMPL fitted, 116/149 CAESAR canonical and 69/149 CAESAR fitted. The two state rows reuse each source value; they are not 596 independent source observations. Joint counts and unknown counts are in waist-fallback-counts.json.

## Agreement and small samples

agreement-extent-waist and agreement-waist-methods are **new descriptive audit calculations**, using existing frozen pairs. Lin CCC uses population moments; Pearson r is reported separately. Bland–Altman differences are target minus source, with limits bias ± 1.96 sample SD (ddof=1). Each state/pathway stratum has one pair per person. Limits are descriptive normal-theory limits, not confidence intervals or evidence of anatomical validity. No new interval is presented as a manuscript result. Degenerate correlations are null. recompute_agreement.py implements this local licensed-input route.

The [NOMO provider record](https://zenodo.org/records/5172126), checked 19 October 2026, displays CC BY metadata while its description prohibits redistribution/modification. That conflict remains unresolved for participant-derived data. Consequently the n=12 paired difference vectors and n=4 repeat vectors are withheld, even shuffled and without identifiers. LSBU approval of the project licence does not resolve provider permission. Frozen paired-effect aggregates and original exploratory intervals, including the repeat diagnostic, are included. recompute_small_n.py accepts separately authorised local vectors and invokes the original 2000-draw method; exact replay also requires original ordering and seed. Shuffling can change finite bootstrap draws. No participant vectors or re-identification mapping are in this release.

## Figures and template

scripts/reproduce_manuscript_figures.py retains the original three manuscript generators' drawing statements and frozen inputs. Its portable SVG renderer reproduces all six original editorial SVGs byte for byte. This route is separate from the companion Matplotlib figures. Figure 2 is a workflow diagram and aggregate fidelity plot; it contains no participant imagery. Figure 6 displays six policies per panel, retaining membership from each full nine-policy grid and the third objective |log(SD ratio)|. It does not compute a new two-dimensional frontier.

The template rendering script uses zero identity/expression and one bound at a time for each of the nine authored controls. It accepts only model/template attachments, never participant parameters or meshes. Plane definitions use the same adaptive selector, 61%-height B1 plane and +0.005 m offset rule. The template illustration is a mechanism demonstration, not an experiment or participant result.

## Selection of the 150 NOMO participants

The frozen study retained 109 usable original identities, replaced one incomplete identity and selected 40 additional distinct eligible identities. The original 110 were therefore not all retained. Additional selection used the released paired-representation eligibility checks and the deterministic source-category-balancing selector in production.NOMO_PAIRED_NICP.core.select_additional (task seed 20261017); source categories were counted before the category-balancing choice. There are 80 female-labelled and 70 male-labelled source records: retained 79/30, replacement 1/0, additions 0/40. These are provider record labels, not sex inferred from geometry, imputed physiology, or independently verified clinical metadata. This is a convenience/availability selection, not a population-representative sample. Fitting histories reused 59 CAESAR fits and allowed 91 additional CAESAR fits with 150 SMPL candidates. Selection does not imply scientific qualification. Linked pathways/states remain within person. Complete waist n=149 and strict shared-height n=103 are analysis eligibility subsets, not new recruited cohorts.
