Incomplete geometry/measurement lineage, uninspected parameters and conditional physical units restrict specific claims, without invalidating unrelated capabilities.

Exact replay establishes reproducibility of a model state, not anatomical fidelity. Visual resolution and existing camera conventions limit inspection. Physical self-contact, regional maps, acquisition times and some condition labels remain unresolved.
